01 Problem

Every AI audit trail is written by the party under audit.

Agents log their own actions. Reports can be wrong, incomplete — or manipulated. A record only the agent produced proves nothing.

02 Method

04 In practice

Three moments it exists for.

The incident

An agent session from three weeks ago is now a postmortem.

Replay the full run — every claimed step against every observed action — instead of scrolling terminal history and guessing.

The audit

"Show us how this system operated in March."

Export a sealed, independently verifiable record — not a policy PDF and a promise.

The drift

An agent did something nobody asked it to.

Unattributed actions surface the moment reality diverges from intent — before they become the incident.
